-----------------------------------------------------
 HOW IT WORKS, AND WHY IT IS BUILT THIS WAY
-----------------------------------------------------

Your resume never leaves your machine. There is no upload, no
server, no account, and nothing is stored anywhere. All the
parsing and matching happens in your own browser with
JavaScript. That is why there is also no per-scan limit — run
it on as many postings as you like.

There is also no "match score". You will not see a number
pretending to measure your chances, because no outside tool can
read an employer's actual configuration. What you get is the
two things that are genuinely verifiable: the keyword gaps for
this specific posting, and the formatting that reliably breaks
parsing.

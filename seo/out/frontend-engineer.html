Frontend Engineer Resume Keywords

前端岗的简历最常栽在一件事上：你写的是『用过 React 的某某替代方案』或者『熟悉 ES6』，而 JD 和解析器要的是原词。前端 JD 里重复出现的词高度集中，这一页把它们列出来，并且给每个词它可能被写成什么样。

The list (20 keywords)

Ordered as a checklist, not by importance — which of these matter depends on the specific posting. The alternative spellings matter: a parser looking for “TypeScript” will not count “TS”.
